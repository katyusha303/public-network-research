# INTRODUCTION

## What are public networks and why are they found everywhere?
Pulic networks are networks that are available in public areas that provide internet access to anyone for free (no password required). Public networks are found nearly everywhere since there presence is linked to any public place of interest(restaurants,malls,parks,etc.) which in turn are present everywhere.

## Public Wifi is inheretly inseucre by design
The reason for this is that almost all public networks don't require a password to establish a connection between the AP(access point) and the client, which was implemented on purpose to provide connection for everyone in that public area. This eventually leads to unencrypted packets being sent across the LAN which can be intercepted,manipulated, or eavsdropped very easily.

## Why this matters
This dilemma is a matter of huge concern since a lot of people that are connected in those public areas perform very important and sensitive digital actions daily(bank transactions,signing in to accounts on different platforms), and performing these actions in insecure networks means unethical hackers can perform many malicious operations in these places such as identity theft,stealing credentials,spreading malware,etc.

## What this research covers
This artice/research/report will cover the attacks that hackers use by abusing the concept of publicly used networks, in addition to defensive techniques that people can undertake to prevent themselves from being a victim of the hackers' malicious objectives.
